Advanced Content Protection 13

Protection features competitors charge for.

Content dripping (scheduled release)

P1

Unlock content N days, weeks or months after the member joined the plan, or on a fixed date. Drip rules can target posts, categories or a whole sequence: rule = plan + content + delay. Compute availability from subscription.start_date. Show a 'available in X days' message, send a notification email when content unlocks, and support drip for LMS lessons. In s2Member free, drip needs PHP; all others charge for it.

PMSSMURMS2

Protected file downloads

P1

Store files in a protected folder (block direct access with .htaccess / nginx rules, or outside webroot), and serve them through a PHP streamer that checks access. Supports download limits per period, inline vs attachment disposition, signed expiring URLs, range requests for audio/video streaming, and Amazon S3 / CloudFront / S3-compatible storage with signed URLs (s2Member). Log every download.

SMPMSS2WPMPP

Global rules by post type / taxonomy / term

P1Partial in MG

PMS sells 'Global Content Restriction': attach rules to a plan, e.g. all posts in category 'Premium' and all 'course' CPT. In SureMembers, ProfilePress and URM this is free (a rules engine).

Free in SM, PP and URM, so MemberGlut should probably keep it in Free.

PMS

Advanced conditional rules (AND / OR / NOT)

P2

Nested condition groups combining plan, role, capability, registration date, field values and more with AND/OR/NOT. Each rule has 'apply to content' and 'except content' lists.

URMRUA

Metered paywall (N free articles)

P2

Guests and free members can read X restricted posts per period before the paywall appears (a 'leaky paywall'). Track views with a cookie and localStorage for guests and user meta for logged-in users. Show a counter banner such as '2 of 3 free articles left'.

PP

Time-based / scheduled rules (Timelock, date restriction)

P3

Enable or disable a rule between dates (RUA Timelock), and restrict content by publish date (RUA Date Restriction). SWPM 'Protect Older Posts': members only see posts published after they joined.

RUA

Restrict by custom field data (ACF / Meta Box)

P3

Restrict posts whose ACF or Meta Box field equals a value (RUA premium add-ons).

RUA

Visibility control (hide everywhere)

P2

Hide restricted content from the blog, search, archives, custom WP_Query lists, sitemaps and the REST API, not just the single view (RUA premium 'Visibility Control'). Also hide restricted terms.

RUA

Block-level restriction (advanced)

P2

SureMembers sells block-level restriction. Members and PMS have it free.

Basic block visibility belongs in Free. In Pro, offer advanced options: plan + schedule + device conditions, and drip on blocks.

SM

Navigation menu filtering by plan

P3

PMS add-on. Free in UM, PP and SM.

Keep in Free.

PMS

Full page protection & partial (section) protection

P3

SWPM add-ons protect the whole page from header to footer, not just the_content, with a template redirect, and protect sections of a page.

SWPM

Google First Click Free / crawler access

P3

Let search engine bots index restricted content (verify the bot by reverse DNS) while users see the paywall. Add paywall structured data (isAccessibleForFree).

SWPM

Login-protected LMS / community spaces

P3

Sell access to FluentCommunity spaces and SureDash portals by plan.

PPSM

Advanced Billing & Monetization 20

The main revenue features in PMS Pro, PP Pro, URM Pro and s2Member Pro.

Additional payment gateways

P1

PayPal Pro / Payflow and ClickBank (s2Member Pro), Authorize.Net (PMS, URM, s2Member), Mollie (PP, URM), Paystack, Razorpay and Paddle (PP). Build a gateway abstraction (AbstractPaymentMethod with webhooks) so each is a module.

PPPMSURMS2

Pro-rated upgrades & downgrades

P1

When switching plans mid-cycle, credit the unused time of the current plan against the new one. Handle both directions, and both recurring and one-time plans. Use Stripe's proration for Stripe subscriptions.

PMSURM

Group / team / corporate memberships (seats)

P1

A buyer purchases N seats and becomes the team owner. They invite members via a shareable link, email or CSV, and members inherit the owner's access. Seat limits per plan, per-seat or tiered seat pricing (URM), a front-end team management page, and removing a member frees the seat. When the owner's subscription ends, all members lose access.

PMSSMURM

Multiple subscriptions per user (per group)

P2

Members can hold one plan from each group at the same time (e.g. Physics Beginner + Math Advanced).

Free in RUA, SM and URM. Decide whether basic multi-plan support belongs in Free.

PMS

Tax & EU VAT

P1

Tax rates by country and state, EU VAT number validation (VIES), reverse charge for B2B, VAT on the invoice, and tax-inclusive or exclusive prices.

Free in ProfilePress.

PMSURMWPUF

Invoices / PDF receipts

P1

Generate PDF invoices for every completed payment with sequential numbering, company details, tax lines and logo. Admins and members can download them, and they are attached to the receipt email.

PMSPPURMWPUF

Multiple currencies (geo or manual)

P2

Show prices in the visitor's local currency, detected by IP geolocation or chosen in a switcher. Set per-currency prices or use an exchange rate.

PMSURM

Pay What You Want / donations

P2

A plan with a minimum price (and an optional suggested price) where the customer enters the amount. Works for one-time and recurring plans.

PMSPP

Fixed-period membership (ends on a date)

P2

The plan expires on a specific calendar date regardless of signup date, e.g. a season pass. PP 'Fixed Subscription Expiration' also allows 'after X duration' for non-recurring plans.

SWPM supports a fixed expiry date in free.

PMSPP

Gift subscriptions

P2

Buy a plan for someone else: enter the recipient's email, delivery date and message. The recipient gets a claim link (or instant activation). Store gift codes and redemption state. s2Member Pro has similar gift/redemption codes.

PMS

Pause subscriptions

P2

Members pause recurring billing for X cycles. Pause the Stripe/PayPal subscription and keep or suspend access according to a setting. Admin limits: max pauses and max duration.

PMS

Order bumps

P3

Add extra plans or products to the same checkout as a one-click upsell, all in one transaction.

PMS

Auto-renewal checkbox

P3

Let the customer choose at checkout whether the plan renews automatically (PMS can force recurring or let the user choose).

PPPMS

Coupons (advanced) & registration discounts

P2

URM and WPUF sell coupons in Pro (free in PMS and PP). Advanced: one coupon per email, auto-apply via URL ?coupon=, and gift/redemption codes (s2Member Pro).

Keep basic coupons in Free and put advanced coupon rules in Pro.

URMS2WPUF

Advanced subscription toolkit

P2

PMS add-on: automatic downgrade to another plan after expiration, a member-count limit per plan (sold out), scheduled availability windows for plans, and more discount options.

PMS

Show member discounts to non-members

P3

WooCommerce: show the members' price next to the regular price ('Members pay $X — join now') to drive signups.

MFW

Custom redirect after payment / offer redirections

P3

s2Member Pro: redirect to a URL per plan after payment, and send to custom offers after login.

S2

One-step pro checkout forms

P2

s2Member Pro-Forms: one on-site form for registration and payment (Stripe, PayPal Pro, Authorize.Net), including 'buy now' and specific-post purchases.

S2

Sell a single post/page (pay-per-content)

P2

Sell access to one specific post or page, or to a custom capability, without a full membership (s2Member Specific Post/Page access with an access window).

S2PP

Pricing fields & dynamic pricing in forms

P3

Pricing radio, checkbox and dropdown fields plus a cart total in forms, letting registration charge for add-ons.
